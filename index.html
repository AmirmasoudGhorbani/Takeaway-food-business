Our Story

A proper kebab, made the way it should be.

Kebab Station Kumeu started with a simple idea: real doner, seasoned in-house and slow-cooked on the vertical spit, shaved to order. No shortcuts, no mystery meat.

We bake our flatbread fresh, prep the salad each morning, and make our sauces by hand — then serve it fast, the way Kumeu likes it.
